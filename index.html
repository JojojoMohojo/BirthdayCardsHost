The Rules

Draw a card if you:

  1. Tom tells you to draw a card — his word is final
  2. Place your drink within a thumb's length of the table
  3. Are caught drinking sparkling wine or prosecco (once per drink)
  4. Wish Tom a happy birthday
  5. Fail to split the G
  6. Take a piss at the pub (first piss per pub is free)

Make someone else draw if you:

  1. Wear the gamer vest for a whole pub
  2. Drink a full glass of milk

Draw one card for each hour you arrive late.
